Privacy Policy & Terms of Service

Last Updated: September 26, 2026
In Short: BlockRig Studio does not collect any personal data, does not require an account, has zero tracking or advertising cookies, and never uploads your skins or 3D models to remote servers. Everything runs 100% on your device.

1. Zero Data Collection & No Tracking

BlockRig Studio is designed as a private, client-side web and mobile utility:

  • No registration, login, account creation, or email address is ever requested.
  • We do not track users, do not run commercial advertising networks, and do not use advertising or tracking cookies.
  • No personal information is collected, stored, transmitted, or sold to third parties.

2. Skins, 3D Models, and Project Files

Any content you import into the studio—including character skins (PNG), 3D assets (GLB/GLTF), and saved project files (.brigmodel):

  • 100% Client-Side Processing: All textures, 3D meshes, posing calculations, and rendering are handled locally inside your browser/device memory using WebGL and Canvas.
  • Your creations, skins, and projects are never transmitted to external cloud servers or stored in any remote database.

3. Mobile Application Permissions (Android)

When utilizing the BlockRig Studio mobile application, permissions are strictly limited to necessary functionality:

  • Storage Access (Save Renders): Used exclusively to save exported high-resolution PNG renders directly into your device's Gallery or Downloads directory.
  • The application does not access your personal photos, contacts, camera, microphone, or location data.

4. Credits & Acknowledgements

Special thanks to McHorse for the original .bobj (Blockbuster OBJ) binary model format and core rendering logic.

The .bobj parsing architecture and Three.js rendering implementation were ported to modern JavaScript by Bay4lly to enable high-performance, real-time Minecraft character posing directly inside web browsers.
